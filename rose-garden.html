Rose Garden

Journaling app inspired by the "rose, bud, thorn" icebreaker. Encouraging users to self-reflect daily with flowers.

Role
Product Designer
Team
4 designers, 7 developers
Timeline
May – June 2025
Tools

Problem Solution Design Decisions Takeaways

Problem

A blank page can be daunting.

For many people, the hardest part of journaling is simply knowing where to begin. It's easy to feel overwhelmed, unsure what to write, or how to organize your thoughts (I knew the feeling all too well).

User Interviews

Through our 27 interviews, several common themes emerged:

A negative stigma around digital journaling.

Users who already journal did not prefer a structured journaling experience.

Users who didn't journal preferred the structure of the rose-bud-thorn.

Our Solution

Make journaling easier and more approachable by using the familiar "rose-bud-thorn" icebreaker. This simple structure offers a gentle guide for daily reflection.

Goals

Leverage digital strengths — Instead of replicating traditional journaling, I wanted to focus on the flexibility of digital spaces, like importing and exporting images, rather than mimicking traditional journal entries.

Balance structure and creative freedom — Provide a guided journaling framework that users can follow or customize, allowing them to shape their experience without feeling restricted.

Design Decisions

Rose – Bud – Thorn

The opening screens guide users into reflection by letting them choose which prompt to begin with, making journaling feel more structured and approachable.

Rose / bud / thorn prompt screen — add final mockup here

Journal Page Design

Inspired by Notability and Instagram Stories, the freeform journal page gives users the flexibility to expand on their thoughts after using the given prompts. Movable text boxes, drawing tools, and image uploads allow each entry to feel personal and unconstrained by a traditional journaling format.
